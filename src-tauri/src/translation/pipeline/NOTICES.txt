1. Sequence matching — derived from Python's difflib

   The similarity ratio and matching blocks in text.rs (`similarity`, `matching_blocks`, `longest_match`) are a
   Rust reimplementation of difflib.SequenceMatcher from CPython 3.12.0 (Lib/difflib.py), so that the translation
   checks compute exactly the ratios they were evaluated with.

   Copyright (c) 2001, 2002, 2003, 2004, 2005, 2006, 2007, 2008, 2009, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016,
   2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023 Python Software Foundation; All Rights Reserved.

   Summary of the changes made (PSF License Agreement, clause 3):
   * translated from Python to Rust, for sequences of characters only;
   * only the configuration without a junk function and without the automatic junk heuristic is implemented, so the
     junk handling and the "popular element" pruning are omitted, and the two junk-only extension loops of the
     longest-match search are removed;
   * only the matching blocks and the ratio are provided (no opcodes, quick ratios, differ or diff output);
   * the algorithm and its tie-breaking are otherwise unchanged.
